Start your survival adventure

Your team has 3 actions each day. Explore a new randomized island each game, gather supplies, craft tools, build a camp, and face the Wheel of Fate. You can even build a raft to reach an unknown island. Survive until rescue or your team runs out of health.

Water is NOT available everywhere. Find freshwater or craft a rain collector. Food and water are consumed each night. Expert mode can scout the surroundings to reveal hidden terrain.

Choose an action

Survival: You get 3 actions per day (unless injured). Physical actions require 15 energy. Each night consumes food and water. Running out of food, water, or health ends the game. Cold islands also drain warmth, and severe hypothermia damages health.

Expert scouting: Open Explore and choose Half-Day Scout (2 actions, 2 energy, reveals N/E/S/W) or Full-Day Scout (3 actions, 10 energy, reveals all 8 adjacent tiles). Scouting reveals terrain but not concealed quicksand. You need sufficient actions and energy, and previously revealed tiles remain visible.
Map: Islands are generated anew. Explorer sees more tiles; Survivor sees adjacent tiles; Expert only sees visited tiles. Survivor and Expert maps contain 1–2 hidden quicksand traps.

Shelters: Build multiple shelters on land (2 wood + 2 cloth each). Each shelter has its own level and permanent map marker. In Build, deposit materials into your current shelter one at a time without using actions, then spend 1 action to finish the upgrade. Resting at a shelter restores more energy (25–65 depending on level); resting without one restores 15. End your day on a shelter tile to gain 5–20 extra energy overnight. Higher shelter levels also reduce cold exposure on Ice Islands. Shelters stay behind when you sail away.

Hunting: Craft a spear or bow, then hunt in suitable terrain. Successful hunts give food and hides; dangerous animals may injure you.

Rafts: Build a raft using 20 wood, 8 rope, and 4 cloth. Launch from a beach in clear or breezy weather with 20 food and 20 water. A new, unknown biome awaits. There is a 1% chance of rescue per voyage.

Rescue: Prepare a signal fire or SOS sign to attract a passing plane. Waving alone will not work. Your adventure ends only when rescued or when your team dies.

Saving: Download a JSON save file and load it next class. Saves from older STRANDED! editions are not compatible with this new island system.
